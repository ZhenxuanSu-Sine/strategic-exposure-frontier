## Current strongest prior-art warning

Halpern & Pass's costly-computation / algorithmic-rationality framework is a direct novelty threat because players choose machines and utility may depend on computation complexity. The agent must inspect their formal complexity functions and determine whether Strategic Exposure is already a special case.

Likewise, expected on-policy resource costs are not new by themselves: constrained MDP / occupation-measure theory and information-constrained Markov control already optimize sequential objectives subject to endogenous expected costs.

The narrower candidate gap to test is:

> keeping game payoff distinct from an implementation-relative resource process, explicitly decomposing provisioned peak capability, mean/tail realized exposure, and **epsilon-best-response opponent-forceable exposure**, then studying the resulting frontier geometry across games and solver architectures.

This statement is a hypothesis about the literature, not a novelty claim. `research/ONLINE_NOVELTY_AUDIT.md` must decide its status.
