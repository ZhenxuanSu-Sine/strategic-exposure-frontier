# Research questions

1. Under what assumptions can peak capability be bounded by expected workload, if ever?
2. How large can `c_peak / c_on` become in finite-horizon games with bounded horizon/action spaces?
3. Which frontier better predicts performance under distribution shift in opponent style: probe, on-policy, or forceable workload?
4. Can payoff-equivalent best responses induce different resource exposure?
5. Can a player trade reward for strategic avoidance of high-cost states, creating a qualitatively new "difficulty routing" dimension?
6. How should capability/workload be aggregated across heterogeneous resources (queries, memory, search) without arbitrary scalarization?
7. Does a resource vector `(peak, average, memory)` explain human/agent skill gaps better than any single axis?
8. In Chess, does node-budget exposure under Stockfish self-play differ substantially from exposure against weaker or adversarially style-selected opponents, after explicitly conditioning on the solver architecture?
